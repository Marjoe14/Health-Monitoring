About the system

Transforming Barangay Garsika's health services

The Smart Community Health Monitoring System replaces paper-based records with a centralized digital platform. BHWs can profile residents, track prenatal and immunization data, monitor BMI, and send SMS alerts — all with offline capability for remote areas.

  • Designed for Barangay Health Workers
  • Works offline with auto-sync
  • Automated SMS notifications
  • Real-time analytics for officials
